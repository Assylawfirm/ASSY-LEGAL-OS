## Legal assessment
1. Written engagement, client identity/capacity, conflicts, scope, expected term and fees must be completed before commencement.
2. 50/25/25 payment structure and ordinary appeal inclusion are the firm's chosen contracting policy, NOT a statutory fee tariff.
3. Third payment must trigger upon appellate disposition OR finality of the first-instance judgment where no ordinary appeal is pursued/available.
4. Enforcement, extraordinary appeals, expert fees, tax and disbursements require explicit case-specific inclusion/exclusion.
5. No promised litigation or collection outcomes, deadlines, or probability guarantees.
6. Settlement, discharge, waiver, acknowledgments, client funds and sub-delegation require appropriate express authority.
7. Early withdrawal, dismissal or compromise fees must be contractually addressed subject to applicable law; do not impose a payment automatically.
8. Client documentation requires least privilege; no public/client data ingestion until approved controls are proven.
9. Separate Egypt legal validation is mandatory; do not copy Saudi terms blindly.
