# REVIEW BATCH 05 — Egyptian Advocacy Law, articles 49–55 and 58
Date: 2026-10-19. Status: verified-from-published-transcriptions / pending original gazette and court archives.

## Legislative review
- Law 147/2019 replaced article 51 paragraph one; added article 51 paragraph two and article 50-bis. Publication reproduced on Egyptian Bar site: https://egyls.com/نص-قانون-المحاماة-بعد-تصديق-الرئيس-علي/
- Article 51(1): investigation of a lawyer or search of law office may be carried out only by prosecution member or investigating judge where permitted by law.
- Article 51(2): in non-flagrante delicto situations, judicial police officers may not detain/arrest an accused lawyer for felony/misdemeanor allegedly committed while exercising right of defense; immediately refer to the First Advocate General of competent appellate prosecution.
- Crucial textual discrepancy: Bar's published 2019 legal transcription displays article 50-bis cross-reference as **(40, 50)** while a news reproduction uses **(49, 50)**. Do not silently correct the published text. Obtain official gazette scan or authentic rectification before assigning final state.
- Article 58: differentiate authorized signature from drafter and review court-level entitlement for pleadings and appeals.

## Cassation case and principle
- Cassation appeal **1649/72**, hearing **2014-06-01**, full transcribed judgment at Egyptian Bar: https://egyls.com/محكمة-النقض-لا-يشترط-أن-يكون-المحامى-ال/
- Holding: the lawyer who signs the initiating pleading need not be the person who drafted it; a duly-qualified lawyer's signature satisfied article 58 even if trainee also drafted/signed.
- Another issue in the same judgment is the limited reach of prohibition in article 8 to deputized counsel; apply only to fact-matched cases and avoid overgeneralization.
- Verification: case number, date and full text supported by Bar reproduction; no direct court-certified judgment acquired in this batch.
